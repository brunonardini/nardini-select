# Home — approved composition

Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: dog owners in Resende, RJ. Goal: understand the curation, learn the categories, and visit or contact the store. Built with Next.js/React, pt-BR copy, no catalog, pricing, checkout, or feed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An open, light institutional presentation in five sections of at least 100vh, with no boxes per pillar and no e-commerce elements.

OWN-WORLD: Light beige, deep brown, and orange. Heavy sans-serif typography, brown/beige/orange illustrations, and original brand imagery. Curation as the language, not a catalog.

STORY: Meet the brand; understand quality, specialization, and curation; explore three commitments; recognize the storefront and open directions; chat on WhatsApp or follow on Instagram.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Continuous panoramic background, text on the left, a separate cut-out Weimaraner on the right, two buttons with down arrows. Mobile keeps the background behind everything and places the dog below the text. No address in the hero.

FORM: Composition explicitly chosen and approved by the user in `design/previews/home-v3-desktop.png` and `home-v3-mobile.png`, with the later revision of the leash in `pilar-premium.png` following the Toh reference. The 100vh height and the content of `design/home-content-v3.md` take precedence over the compressed proportions of the rasters. No random draw was used: the direction came from explicit approval.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
