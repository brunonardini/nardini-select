## Users

Dog owners in Resende, RJ, discovering the brand, learning what the store sells, and planning a visit or contacting the shop.

## Product Purpose

Nardini Select is a pet shop in Resende, RJ, focused on dog owners. The current website presents the brand, the store's categories, and the physical location. Success means a visitor understands the store's offer and can visit or contact it.

## Positioning

A specialized pet shop offering carefully curated products for pets' well-being, behavioral development, and quality of life. Selection combines quality, safety, functionality, and design for discerning owners and pet professionals. Brand essence: quality, specialization, and curation. Main message: "Uma seleção especial para uma vida mais saudável, equilibrada e feliz ao lado do seu pet."

## Operating Context

- The current home introduces the brand first, then what the store sells, then where it is and how to get there. WhatsApp provides direct contact with the shop.
- Market: Resende, RJ, Brazil. Language: Portuguese (pt-BR). No prices in the current scope.
- Prioritize mobile visitors browsing locally and opening directions or WhatsApp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No cart, payment, or account system. WhatsApp is the contact channel.
- No product catalog, individual product listings, or prices in the current site scope.
- Categories: professional training materials; natural treats; natural food; toys; hygiene and care products; bowls and accessories.
- Do not render the phone number in visible text, footer, or storefront imagery. Use WhatsApp buttons labeled "Fale com a gente". Conceal the small printed phone strip in the storefront image displayed by the website, preserving the original source. A direct WhatsApp link does not make the destination number secret or inaccessible; do not claim it does.
- Place prominent WhatsApp and Instagram contact actions after the location/address section, in two open columns on desktop and stacked on mobile. No Instagram feed in the current scope.
- Use an address-based directions link. Do not invent local landmarks, routes, store hours, delivery coverage, or a map pin that has not been verified.
- Hosting/deploy target remains undecided.

## Product Principles

1. **Curation over volume.** Fewer, chosen products, each presented with a reason to trust the pick.
2. **Visit or contact the shop.** The current institutional home helps visitors locate the physical store and talk to it on WhatsApp.
3. **Built for Brazilian pet owners.** Natural pt-BR copy, BRL, and local expectations, not a translated template.
4. **Mobile-first reality.** The phone in hand is the primary context for discovering the store, opening directions, and contacting it.
